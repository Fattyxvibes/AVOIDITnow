# AVOIDITnow Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 2026  
**Last updated:** August 2026

## 1. Introduction

AVOIDITnow operates the AVOIDITnow website, product-checking tools, source-linked updates, Islamic guidance features, and related public services (collectively, the “**Services**”). The public product checker, updates, and source pages do not require visitor registration.

This Privacy Policy explains the limited information AVOIDITnow may handle when you use the Services, contact us, submit a voluntary Islamic-guidance question, or use a product search. It also explains the choices and rights that may be available under applicable law.

AVOIDITnow is a public educational and awareness platform. Its product-checking and alternatives information is source-linked and may change over time. This Privacy Policy addresses information practices; it does not make a statement about the accuracy, safety, availability, religious status, or suitability of any product, service, source, or alternative.

## 3. Information We Handle

AVOIDITnow does not require public visitors to create an account or provide a name, email address, or identity to use the product checker, updates, or source pages.

| Category | What may be handled | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Product-search analytics | A normalised product-search term and the time of the search | To produce aggregate counts of popular and less-searched product terms for the private owner dashboard and improve the product checker. This analytics record does not store an account ID, email address, IP address, device identifier, session identifier, or other searcher identity. |
| Contact communications | Your email address, message content, attachments, and follow-up correspondence | Only when you choose to email us; used to respond, manage the request, and protect our rights. |
| Islamic guidance prompts | Questions and information you voluntarily include in a guidance request, plus the generated response | To generate the requested educational response and maintain safe, reliable operation. Do not include sensitive personal, financial, government, health, or third-party information. |
| Essential technical and security information | Limited server error, abuse-prevention, and security information generated in operating the website | To deliver, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the Services. For public product checks and Islamic guidance, this may include a short-lived, one-way keyed identifier derived from a network address; we do not retain the raw IP address in these controls. |
| Essential local preferences | The local preference that records whether you accepted the site’s policy acknowledgement | To avoid repeatedly displaying the acknowledgement in the same browser. |

### Information We Do Not Request for Public Use

AVOIDITnow does not ask public visitors for registration details, payment-card details, government identifiers, precise location information, medical information, or other highly sensitive information to use the public product checker, updates, or source pages. Please do not include such information in contact messages or Islamic-guidance questions.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use the limited information above only to:

1. provide, operate, maintain, and secure the Services;
2. return a requested educational Islamic-guidance response when you voluntarily submit a question;
3. respond to messages, privacy requests, corrections, copyright notices, or other inquiries;
4. measure aggregate product-search activity using normalised product terms and timestamps only, without creating visitor profiles; and
5. detect, prevent, investigate, and address fraud, misuse, security incidents, prohibited conduct, and technical errors.

Where a law requires a legal basis for processing, AVOIDITnow may rely on a requested service, legitimate interests such as security and service improvement, consent where required, and compliance with legal obligations. The appropriate basis depends on the activity and jurisdiction.

## 7. Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was handled, including security, legal, dispute-resolution, and record-keeping purposes.

| Information type | Retention approach |
|---|---|
| Product-search analytics | Retained as normalised product terms and timestamps only while needed for aggregate trend reporting and product-checker improvement. |
| Abuse-prevention counters and guidance-stream leases | Made unusable automatically when their short expiry passes and periodically removed. ## 8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

AVOIDITnow may use essential local storage, session storage, cookies, or similar technologies needed for basic site operation, security, preferences, and error prevention. The policy-acknowledgement preference is stored locally in the visitor’s browser so the notice is not repeatedly displayed.

We will not activate non-essential analytics, advertising, or similar tracking technologies where prior consent is required unless we provide a clear consent mechanism and a way to withdraw or change that choice.
