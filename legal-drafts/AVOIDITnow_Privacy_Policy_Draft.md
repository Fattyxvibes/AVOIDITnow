## 4. How We Use Personal Data

We use personal data only for legitimate, disclosed purposes, including to:

1. provide, operate, maintain, and secure the Services;
2. authenticate users and administer accounts;
3. display, moderate, investigate, or remove community contributions where community features are enabled;
4. generate an educational response when you submit an Islamic guidance question;
5. respond to messages, privacy requests, corrections, copyright notices, or other inquiries;
6. detect, prevent, investigate, and address fraud, misuse, security incidents, prohibited conduct, and technical errors;
7. measure and improve the reliability, accessibility, and usability of the Services, subject to applicable cookie and consent rules;
8. comply with legal obligations, enforce our Terms of Use, establish or defend legal claims, and protect the rights, safety, and security of AVOIDITnow, users, and others; and
9. administer donations if and when that functionality is formally launched.

Where a law requires a legal basis for processing, AVOIDITnow may rely on performance of a contract or requested service, legitimate interests such as security and service improvement, consent where required, and compliance with legal obligations. The appropriate basis depends on the processing activity and jurisdiction.

1. **Service providers and processors** that host, secure, authenticate, operate, support, or maintain the Services, subject to appropriate contractual and security obligations;
2. **Authentication providers**, where you choose to sign in through them;
3. **Model or technology providers**, to the extent needed to process a voluntary Islamic-guidance request or operate a related feature;
4. **Payment providers**, only if a donation or payment feature is launched and you choose to use it;
5. **Professional advisers, insurers, auditors, regulators, courts, or law-enforcement bodies** where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or legal claims;
6. **A successor organisation** in connection with a permitted reorganisation, merger, acquisition, or transfer of assets, subject to applicable law; and
7. **The public**, only for content you choose to post or submit to a public community feature. Do not include personal data in public content that you do not want others to see.

Our Services may link to source publishers, social platforms, payment providers, and other third-party websites. Their data practices are governed by their own notices and terms, not this Privacy Policy.
