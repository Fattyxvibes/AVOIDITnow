# Lightweight Hero Video Validation

Date: 2026-08-22

The previous 2560 × 1440 hero asset was 55,065,324 bytes at approximately 6.46 Mb/s. The replacement remains 2560 × 1440 with stereo AAC audio and progressive-start metadata, while reducing delivery to 25,159,392 bytes at approximately 2.95 Mb/s. This lowers the total asset size by about 54% and lowers the required video throughput by about 55%.

Desktop review confirms that the hero now exposes only a clear sound control on the top-right of the video; no play/pause button remains. Mobile review confirms that the single sound control remains visible in the contained video area and does not obstruct the header, hero copy, product search, or panel text. The component retains muted inline autoplay and looping, enabling browser-compatible automatic playback while allowing visitors to turn audio on deliberately.
