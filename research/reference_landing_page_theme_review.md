# Reference Landing-Page Theme Review

Date reviewed: 2026-08-23

The referenced site uses three non-proprietary landing-page themes that are appropriate to re-express in Avoidit’s own voice: purposeful consumer choice, a peace-and-justice mission, and visible boycott-list impact figures. It also separates its public-interest aims from the directory and donation actions.

Avoidit will not reproduce the reference site’s wording, visual design, Gaza casualty figures, or unverified audience claims. The existing three-card landing-page strip is the appropriate low-impact place to communicate original Avoidit mission and database scale: 220+ reviewed companies, 600+ affected products, and a clearly labelled aspiration to reach 1.5 million people for peace advocacy.

Sources reviewed: https://boycott-israel.org/ and https://boycott-israel.org/about.html
