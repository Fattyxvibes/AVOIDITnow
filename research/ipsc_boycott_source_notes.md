# Ireland Palestine Solidarity Campaign boycott source notes

Reviewed on 14 August 2026 for the Avoid It product checker.

Primary source: https://www.ipsc.ie/campaigns/consumer-boycott

## Use in product-checker

The source describes a consumer boycott of Israeli goods and services and companies it identifies as connected to Israel's occupation and apartheid. It warns that its list is **not exhaustive**, because brands and companies change. The website must therefore present results as a match against **reviewed IPSC-referenced entries**, rather than a complete global determination.

The source identifies a barcode prefix of 729 as an Israel barcode indicator while also warning that it does not appear on all Israeli products; it should only be displayed as a cautionary clue, not as a conclusive product classification.

## Product and company categories described by IPSC

The source includes supermarket produce and brands, homeware and hardware, cosmetics and clothing, pharmaceuticals, children's products, online tools and services, and machinery, insurance and energy. Examples named on the reviewed page include Mehadrin/Jaffa, Carmel-Agrexco, Tivall, Sabra/Strauss Group, SodaStream, Keter, Palram, Ahava, Teva, Wix, HP, Motorola Solutions, Siemens, Caterpillar, JCB, and Hyundai.

## Product governance requirements

1. Results must name IPSC as the source and link to the source page.
2. The source page is Ireland-focused; regional availability and alternatives must be labelled as administrator-reviewed, with a timestamp and a source link.
3. The interface must include the source's non-exhaustive-list caveat and should never claim that an unlisted product is definitively not boycotted.
4. Any alternative recommendation must identify its region, source, and review date.
