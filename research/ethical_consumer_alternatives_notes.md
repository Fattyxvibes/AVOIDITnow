# Ethical Consumer alternatives source notes

Reviewed on 14 August 2026 for the Avoid It regional-alternatives workflow.

Primary source: https://www.ethicalconsumer.org/ethicalcampaigns/boycotts/alternative-ethical-brands-support-palestine

Ethical Consumer describes its article as a list of brands in the same sectors as major boycott targets that it considers among the more ethical options according to its corporate ratings. It says that, at minimum, those alternatives are less complicit in Palestinian human-rights abuses than brands facing boycott calls and are not being targeted by Palestinian human-rights campaigners. It also notes that some have taken active steps to support Palestine.

The source’s alternatives are sector-specific and predominantly researched for UK consumers. Examples visible in the article include UK travel alternatives such as Hostelling Scotland, Canopy & Stars, Co-op Travel, Independent Hostels UK, and The Landmark Trust for specified travel-booking targets. The source offers sections for several targets, including Siemens and SodaStream, but individual alternative entries must be read and attributed separately before being added to the site.

## Product-governance requirements

1. Label Ethical Consumer entries as **UK-focused source research**, not global availability or a universal ethical certification.
2. Link the published page and retain a review date for every derived alternative.
3. Use the source’s own qualifications: an alternative is an administrator-reviewed candidate, not a definitive claim that it is non-boycotted.
4. Do not represent paywalled or inaccessible detailed ratings as though the site independently verified them.

## SodaStream section observed

The published SodaStream section states that Ethical Consumer has not rated alternative brands to SodaStream. It says that CO2 You appears to be a small, independent company that allows consumers to recycle their CO2 cylinders, and that Philips is recommended in its batteries guide (2023). It also names Aarke and DrinkMate as brands not targeted by an official BDS boycott call.

Any interface record derived from this section must therefore say **“source-listed candidate; not an independent Avoid It certification”**, preserve the original source link and article date (5 August 2026), and use a UK-focused availability caveat. It must not convert “not targeted by an official BDS boycott call” into a universal claim that a company is non-boycotted or ethically approved.
