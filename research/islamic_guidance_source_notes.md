# Islamic guidance source notes

Reviewed on 14 August 2026 for the Avoid It Islamic guidance assistant.

## Quran reference resource

Primary public reference: https://quran.com/en

The reviewed page provides multilingual Qur'an reading, searching, translations, tafsir, recitations, and word-by-word study features. The assistant can provide a Quran.com link when it references a Qur'an passage, but must name the translation or source when it quotes wording.

## Hadith reference resource

Primary public reference: https://sunnah.com/

The reviewed page provides searchable public access to major hadith collections, including Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, the Sunan collections, Muwatta Malik, Musnad Ahmad, and selected works. The assistant can use a Sunnah.com link for a cited narration.

## Assistant boundary

The assistant must describe itself as general educational guidance. It must not present a conclusive fatwa, claim scholarly consensus without a cited basis, or replace consultation with a qualified and trusted scholar or local imam—especially for personal, legal, financial, marriage, medical, or high-stakes questions.

Every response should separate: (1) a concise educational answer, (2) cited Quran or hadith references when a reliable reference is available, and (3) a short note when legitimate scholarly disagreement may exist.
